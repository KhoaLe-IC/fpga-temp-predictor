Our proposed outcome is a three-hour-ahead temperature forecast from past hourly values, with coefficients trained in C++ and fixed-point inference on an FPGA. We will provide two kinds of evidence: forecast quality against baselines on unseen data, and RTL correctness against the fixed-point reference. We would welcome feedback on the three-hour scope, the FPGA board and tools, and the minimum expected board demonstration. Humidity or pressure would be added only after the core work is complete.
