We plan to use hourly NASA POWER T2M data, which is gridded data for a selected coordinate rather than a sensor reading at an exact point. We will confirm the coordinate, date range, time convention, units, and any missing or duplicate hours before creating samples. The value at t minus twenty-one hours is the same hour on the previous day relative to the target at t plus three hours. A buffer of twenty-five consecutive samples covers t minus twenty-four through t; the future target is used only as a training or evaluation label.
